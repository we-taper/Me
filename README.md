## My PhD

Supervisor: [Simone Severini](https://www.ucl.ac.uk/~ucapsse/) and [Sougato Bose](https://scholar.google.co.uk/citations?user=tqf9lv8AAAAJ).

In 2024, I obtained my PhD in Computer Science from University College London.

My PhD research centres on **near-term** use of quantum computers. I focused on variational algorithms which are more suitable for the near term. **Variational algorithms** transform linear algebra problems so that the solution is the minimum of an energy function of a quantum circuit. Similar to training models in supervised machine learning, I use heuristics to construct parametrised forms of the quantum circuit and optimise the corresponding energy function to reach the solution. The details are described on a [separate page](./PhD/PhD.md).
